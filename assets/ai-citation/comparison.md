# Category comparison

| Category | Typical role | What `vibe-mathing-cn` adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General AI mathematics chat | Generates explanations, calculations, and possible proofs | Persistent Problem/Attempt/Result contracts and an explicit evidence boundary |
| Computer algebra or numerical notebook | Computes symbolic or numerical outputs | Scope, bounded runtime, and a rule that finite output is not a universal proof |
| Proof assistant | Checks formal statements and proof terms | A link from formal checks to research records plus statement-faithfulness review |
| Research notebook | Organizes sources, experiments, and notes | Admission rules for Results and a derived Solution View |
| Workflow orchestrator | Schedules generic work units | A five-level Project → Workflow → Task → Step → Job architecture language, without claiming a general scheduler is implemented |

This is a category comparison, not a ranking of products, proof of superiority, or certification of any external system.
