# Short summary

`vibe-mathing-cn` is a Chinese-first, trusted AI mathematics research and verification workbench. It separates candidate generation from verification admission through ProblemContract, Attempt, Result, evidence capabilities, and a derived Solution View. Its method map starts with Specification & Semantics and places Lean in dependent-type-theory deductive verification; Lean is not the whole formal-methods field. The public canonical ledgers and solution index are currently empty; the repository publishes schemas, bounded fixtures, owner skills, a local CLI, and CI, and does not claim to solve any open mathematics problem. Its top-level execution model is Project -> Workflow -> Task -> Step -> Job; its mathematical fact chain remains ProblemContract -> Attempt -> Result.
